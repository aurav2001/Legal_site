Our Expertise

Comprehensive legal solutions tailored to high-stakes corporate disputes, Supreme Court appellate matters, cross-border transactions, and global arbitrations.

At Vanguard & Partners, we offer strategic legal counsel across three core pillars: Courtroom Litigation before Supreme Court & High Courts, Alternate Dispute Resolution (Arbitration & Mediation), and Corporate Restructuring & Regulatory Practice.

Pillar I

Litigation across Courts & Tribunals

Our advocates command formidable courtroom stature before the Supreme Court of India, Delhi High Court, Bombay High Court, and specialized tribunals including NCLAT, APTEL, TDSAT, and the National Green Tribunal.

  • ✦ Civil & Commercial Litigation
  • ✦ Constitutional Law & Special Leave Petitions (SLPs)
  • ✦ Criminal Law & White-Collar Defense (PMLA / ED / CBI)
  • ✦ Environmental Law & National Green Tribunal (NGT)
  • ✦ Regulatory Litigation (Power, Telecommunications, Mining)
Pillar II

Alternate Dispute Resolution (ADR)

We represent multinational corporations, sovereign contractors, and infrastructure developers in high-value cross-border arbitrations administered under SIAC, ICC, LCIA, and UNCITRAL rules.

  • ✦ Domestic & International Commercial Arbitration
  • ✦ Section 9 & Section 34 Applications (Setting Aside Awards)
  • ✦ Cross-Border Enforcement under the New York Convention 1958
  • ✦ Mediation & Pre-Arbitral Dispute Escalation
Pillar III

Corporate & Commercial Practice

End-to-end strategic advisory on corporate restructurings, insolvencies, mergers, antitrust clearances, and regulatory filings under Indian corporate legislation.

  • ✦ Corporate Insolvency & Restructuring (IBC 2016)
  • ✦ Mergers, Demergers & Capital Reduction (NCLT)
  • ✦ Competition & Antitrust Law (CCI Clearance)
  • ✦ Securities Law & SEBI Takeover Regulations
  • ✦ Direct & Indirect Taxation Advisory
